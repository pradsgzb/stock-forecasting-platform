# ADR 0014 — Restrict Section 15 to next-session paper backtesting

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-03

## Context

A backtest can appear profitable when it fills at prices that existed before a signal was
available, ignores transaction costs, assumes unlimited liquidity, or silently changes its
inputs. Broker connectivity would introduce financial, operational, security, and regulatory
risks beyond this release.

## Decision

The engine is paper-only and exposes no broker interface. A forecast for date T can execute no
earlier than the next supplied session open and only if it was generated by that open. The
engine applies explicit spread, slippage, commission, volume participation, cash, position,
gross exposure, turnover, and drawdown limits.

Backtest results are immutable and content-addressed. Their fingerprints exclude generated
run, order, and fill IDs but include economic fills, positions, equity, metrics, configuration,
and signal identity.

## Consequences

- Results are more conservative than same-close or frictionless simulations.
- The initial implementation is long-only and daily-bar based.
- Shorting, borrow availability, taxes, corporate-event cashflows, intraday order books, and
  live execution require separate designs.
- A profitable paper result is evidence for further research, not proof of future returns.
